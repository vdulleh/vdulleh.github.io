Manifesto

Photography is not about what you see.

It is about what you feel when the light breaks, when texture fights back, and when silence says more than expression.

The visual language is deliberately brutalist: minimal chrome, rigid structure, image-first storytelling, and a red signal line that cuts through the darkness only when it matters.
